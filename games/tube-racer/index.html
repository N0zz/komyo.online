Tube Racer

Roll around the inside of a giant pipe at Mach 2 — find the gap, then cool the engine.

How to play

You are magnetically stuck to the inside of a huge pipe, so there is no left or right edge — you roll all the way around the wall to line up with the gap in each barrier. Hold boost to accelerate past Mach 1 and score faster, but boosting heats the engine, and a full heat gauge destroys the bike; steer onto the glowing blue cooling strips to shed that heat. Play endless Classic, race a 30 km Sprint against the clock, survive Redline with the boost jammed on, or take it easy in Cruise.
