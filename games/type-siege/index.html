Type Siege

Doodle armies march on your keep — type the word above each one to cut it down. Eight languages.

How to play

Sketched soldiers march across the notebook page and lay siege to your keep, each carrying a word. Type its first letter and the game locks onto the nearest matching enemy — finish the word and it falls. They do not charge in and vanish: swordsmen halt at the wall and hammer it, archers hold back and shoot, and trebuchets lob rocks marked with tiny urgent words, so the longer you leave a word unfinished the more attackers pile up. Words come from your choice of eight languages, with accents optional; play eight authored stages, an endless siege, or Rain, where words fall from the top of the page.
