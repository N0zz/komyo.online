Chrome Runner

Polished chromium dino — jump the cacti, duck the gantries, chase a faster 1000 m.

How to play

Run right and never stop: tap or press Space to jump the cacti, hold Down to duck under the low steel gantries and the birds. Squeezing past an obstacle grants a slipstream surge that speeds you up, the pace climbs the further you get, and the sky rolls from bright day through dusk into a starry night. Endless runs until you clip something; Sprint is a 1000 m time trial where every clip costs you a stumble.
