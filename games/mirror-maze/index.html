Mirror Maze

Spin the mirrors, split the beam, light every target in its own colour. A neon laser puzzle.

How to play

A laser fires into the grid from one edge. Tap any mirror to flip it between / and \ and bend the beam ninety degrees — light every target in the exact colour it asks for and the maze is solved. A prism splits white light: red bends left, green carries straight on, blue bends right. Filters only let one colour channel through, and a combiner adds the beams that reach it, so red plus green makes yellow. Beams crossing at right angles simply pass through each other. Play twelve campaign mazes, one shared daily maze, or an endless run of ever bigger grids.

HOW THE PIECES WORK

LASER Fires white light into the grid.

MIRROR Tap it to flip / and \. Bends the beam 90°. Corner arrow = you can turn it.

TARGET Needs the colour of its letter. Empty ring = dark, filled + tick = lit.

PRISM Splits white light: red bends left, green goes straight, blue bends right.

FILTER Only its own colour gets through. Anything else stops here.

COMBINER Adds the beams that arrive and fires the mix along its arrow. Red + green = yellow.

WALL Blocks the beam. Riveted pieces never turn.

BEAM One colour draws a solid line, two mixed colours a dashed line, white a double line. Rings match.

HINT HINT — the ringed mirror is facing the wrong way. Ask again for a different one.
