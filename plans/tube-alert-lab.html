WHAT THE RESEARCH ACTUALLY SAYS

  • Urgency comes from four knobs (Edworthy/Loxley/Dennis 1991): faster rate, higher pitch, irregular harmonics, and a sharp attack. Rate and pitch are the strongest — and this game already spends the rate knob (tempo goes 0.28 s → 0.11 s), so the per-blip design should spend pitch and harmonic content, not add more events.
  • Pulses, not tones. The medical standard builds every alarm from short pulses (~75–200 ms, soft-ish attack/decay in the 7.5–100 ms range) grouped into a burst: 3 pulses for medium priority, 10 for high. The burst is the message; the pulse is just its carrier.
  • Harmonically rich, mid-band. The fundamental sits 150–1000 Hz with at least four or five harmonics between 150 Hz and 4 kHz, within ~15 dB of each other. That is why real alarms cut through noise where a pure sine does not — and it is exactly what a bare square wave fails at.
  • Cockpit practice separates by grammar, not volume: a single chime means caution, a continuous repetitive chime means warning, and a few alerts get their own melody (the "cavalry charge", the C-chord) so they are identifiable without looking.
  • Fire alarms are one pattern worldwide (ISO 8201 T-3: three 0.5 s pulses, 1.5 s gap, forever) — proof that a rhythm is more recognisable than a timbre.

GROUP 1 · COCKPIT FAMILY identifiable without looking

Airbus-style alerting: the sound is a chime — a struck, harmonically rich body with a fast attack and a decaying tail — and the message lives in how often it repeats. This is the closest match to what Tube Racer already does, because the game is already coding urgency in tempo.

GROUP 2 · STANDARDS-SHAPED PULSES IEC 60601-1-8 / ISO 8201

Pulse-and-burst construction, built to the standards' shape rather than their letter: mid-band fundamental, a stack of harmonics, softened attack so it does not click, grouped into recognisable rhythms. These are the least fatiguing per unit of urgency, which matters when it fires nine times a second.

GROUP 3 · MACHINE-ROOM it is a pipe, not an airliner

The same principles pointed at the game's own fiction: a hot metal tube at Mach 1.7. Harmonically irregular (the strongest urgency knob after rate), lower fundamental, more industrial. Riskier — irregular partials are urgent and tiring.
