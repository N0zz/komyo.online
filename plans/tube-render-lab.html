What to do: freeze the roll (set YOUR ROLL to 0) and watch a far obstacle. If it still crawls sideways as it approaches, that is not a mechanic — it's the offset clamp in ctr(). Each obstacle is drawn as one wedge between two arcs on two independently clamped centres, and the near end's clamp tightens as it comes at you, so the wedge shears a little more every frame. Sliding sideways while growing is exactly "rolling along the pipe towards us".
